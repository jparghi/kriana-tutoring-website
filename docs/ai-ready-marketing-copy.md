# AI-Ready Young Engineers Marketing Copy

## Positioning

The active Smartivo, Bricks Challenge and Algo Play programs build foundations
that are relevant to robotics, automation and AI. They are not presented as
formal artificial-intelligence or machine-learning courses.

## Campaign

- Headline: **Build. Code. Think Ahead.**
- Supporting line: **Hands-on engineering and coding programs that build the
  logic, creativity and problem-solving skills children need for an AI-powered
  future.**
- Badge: **Coding • Robotics • AI-Ready Foundations**
- Short tagline: **Future-ready skills start with hands-on learning.**
- CTA: **Explore Young Engineers Programs**

## Program positioning

### Smartivo

- Eyebrow: **Early Coding Foundations**
- Tags: **Sequencing · Coding Logic · AI-Ready Thinking**
- Copy: Children bring robots to life through playful coding missions while
  building sequencing, logic and computational-thinking foundations.

### Bricks Challenge

- Eyebrow: **Engineering & Problem-Solving**
- Tags: **Mechanics · Design Thinking · Creative Problem-Solving**
- Copy: Children build and test mechanical models while developing engineering
  thinking, creativity and systematic problem-solving.

### Algo Play

- Eyebrow: **Algorithms & Coding**
- Tags: **Algorithms · Debugging · Robotics & AI Foundations**
- Copy: Children explore algorithms, loops, conditions and debugging—the coding
  logic behind robotics, automation and future AI technologies.

## Social captions

### Full caption

Future-ready skills start with hands-on learning. Through building, coding,
testing and debugging, children develop the logical and creative thinking behind
robotics, automation and tomorrow's AI technologies. Explore Smartivo, Bricks
Challenge and Algo Play at Kriana Tutoring.

### Short caption

Build. Code. Think Ahead. Explore hands-on Young Engineers programs that develop
coding, engineering and AI-ready foundations.

## Claim guardrails

Do not advertise the active programs as AI classes, ML classes, generative-AI
classes or model-training courses. New claims require an approved curriculum
covering the program, ages, outcomes, tools and instructor requirements.
