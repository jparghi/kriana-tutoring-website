Instructor Obligations
• Enforcing zero-tardiness policy is a
must. Tardiness is dealt seriously by
students, parents, and course
coordinator.
• Build a close relationship with the
course coordinator by having short
meetings before and after each
lessons.
• Create meaningful connections
with the parents.
• Offer lesson “promos” to the
students.
• Ensure proper upkeep of your
teaching equipment by closely
monitoring it and promptly
collecting any pieces that may fall
on the floor.
• Do not take photos of the children
without parental consent.
• Check the attendance at the
beginning of each lessons.
• Make sure to come to the lesson
fully prepared. Familiarize yourself
with the lesson plan thoroughly
and become proficient in building
the model for that specific lesson.-- Reminders:
• Prioritize helping the students who
haven't completed the basic model,
particularly those progressing at a
slower pace.
• Unclear lesson checkpoints: decide
if students should show the instructor
their complete stages at a designated
location or if the instructor will come
to their seats to assess their progress.
• Be sure to dress appropriately
• Do not smoke
in front of the children.
• Do not speak on the phone
during class.
• Use of profane language
is strictly prohibited!
• Do not dismiss the students before
the scheduled end of the lesson.
Reminders:
• Don't leave the student unattended
until they have been picked up.
• Do not remove disruptive students
from the classroom without supervision.
• Be alert and pay attention
to what is going on in class.
• In the event of disciplinary issues
or exceptional incidents, at the
study center, be sure to update the
relevant parties and clarify your
conditions
to continue attending the class.
Working
Procedures
Proper conduct
with the POS
• Keep the classroom clean and in order.
• Keep the classroom equipment intact
and do not unnecessarily use study
facilities.
• At schools - often ask students to lift
chairs onto the tables (if required).
• Be polite and kind to the course
coordinator and maintenance personnel.
• Classroom visibility and suitability
to the course are essential.
Working
Procedures
Conduct
with the Parents
• Be kind to parents as it contributes to the overall well-
being and success of students.
• Get to know the student’s parents as it improves
communication and collaboration.
• Encourage parents to join the lesson; they are welcome
to arrive 15 minutes before the end of the lesson, see
the model and assist in disassembling the model.
• Be understanding if parents express concerns. It is
essential to show respect through active listening and
acknowledging their perspectives.
• After the end of each lessons, we maintain an open line
of communication to parents by sending SMS updates,
providing valuable insights into their child's progress
and activities during the class.
• Encourage the parents to visit the Young Engineers
website.
