# Kriana Tutoring — Animated Explainer Storyboard Scripts

**Total runtime:** 60–90 seconds  
**Format:** 2D animated characters, no real people required  
**Recommended tools:** Vyond · Animaker · Adobe After Effects · professional 2D studio

---

## Scene 01 · 0–10s · Every Child Learns Differently

**Visual:**  
Three animated children sit at the same desk with identical worksheets. One looks confident, one confused, one staring out the window. A question mark floats above each child.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Every Child Learns Differently.

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "Every child is different. They think differently. They learn differently. They grow differently."

**Animation Notes:**  
Gentle fade-in. Each child appears one at a time with a staggered delay. Question marks animate in with a soft bounce. Warm, inviting colour palette.

---

## Scene 02 · 10–25s · The Problem with One-Size-Fits-All

**Visual:**  
A conveyor belt moves children past a teacher handing each one an identical worksheet. Children look defeated. Report cards float down showing D- grades.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> One plan. One worksheet. One result.

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "When every child gets the same plan, most children fall behind. Not because they can't learn — but because no one built a plan around how they learn."

**Animation Notes:**  
Conveyor belt scrolls left to right continuously. Report cards flutter down like falling leaves. Entire scene is desaturated (muted greys) to convey the problem. Slow, heavy music.

---

## Scene 03 · 25–40s · The Kriana Assessment

**Visual:**  
A tutor sits with a child at a warm wooden table. Gentle amber light enters from the left. The tutor holds a clipboard and smiles. Three subject icons (Math +, Reading book, Writing pencil) light up one by one as the child answers.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Step 1: Assessment — Know Before You Plan

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "At Kriana, we start by getting to know your child. Before a single lesson, we run a thorough assessment — to understand exactly where they are, and how they learn best."

**Animation Notes:**  
Warm amber radial glow pulses softly in the background. Icons scale and brighten sequentially with a 0.5s delay each. Tutor nods encouragingly. Music brightens.

---

## Scene 04 · 40–55s · The Personalized Learning Plan

**Visual:**  
A document/scroll unfolds on screen labelled with the child's name. Custom plan items appear one by one beside coloured dots. A glowing teal path traces forward from the document toward the horizon.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Step 2: Your Child's Personalized Learning Plan

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "Then we build a plan made for your child — not for every child. A personalized learning path that starts where they are, and takes them where they need to go."

**Animation Notes:**  
Scroll unfurls from the top. Each plan item fades up with staggered timing. Teal path uses stroke-dashoffset animation to trace forward. Arrow appears at path end.

---

## Scene 05 · 55–70s · Monthly Progress & Parent Updates

**Visual:**  
A progress bar fills across the screen in teal. Four subject bars fill simultaneously. A phone notification slides in from the right — "Progress Update Ready." Two figures high-five in the background.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Monthly Progress Reviews · Parent Updates Included

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "Every month, we review your child's progress. And you're always kept in the loop — because when parents and tutors work together, children thrive."

**Animation Notes:**  
Progress bar animates left to right over 2.5s. Subject bars animate with a stagger. Phone notification uses slide-in from right with a bounce. Notification badge pops with a scale animation.

---

## Scene 06 · 70–82s · Confidence & Academic Growth

**Visual:**  
The same three children from Scene 01 — but now all confident. Worksheets are filled in with checkmarks. Stars burst from each child. The previously confused child raises their hand enthusiastically.

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Confident Learners. Real Results.

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "The result? Children who believe in themselves. Who walk into class ready. Who stop saying 'I can't' — and start saying 'I've got this.'"

**Animation Notes:**  
Scene transitions from desaturated (matching Scene 02 tone) to vibrant full colour as children appear. Star polygons burst with scale + rotate animation. Raised arms animate last. Upbeat music swells to peak.

---

## Scene 07 · 82–90s · Call to Action

**Visual:**  
"KRIANA" types onto screen in large bold text. Ottawa skyline silhouette fades in behind at low opacity. "Personalized Learning For Every Child." appears below. A teal CTA button pulses — "Book a Free Assessment."

**On-Screen Text:**  
> Kriana Tutoring · Personalized Learning For Every Child  
> Book a Free Assessment — krianatutoring.com

**Voiceover Script:**  
> "Kriana Tutoring. Personalized learning for every child. Book your free assessment today — and let's build a plan around your child."

**Animation Notes:**  
"KRIANA" uses a type-in letter-spacing animation. Ottawa skyline fades in at 18% opacity. CTA button uses a pulsing box-shadow glow. Music resolves warmly. Hold last frame 2s before fade out.

## Production Notes

- **Voice talent:** Warm, friendly female voice. Canadian or neutral North American accent preferred.
- **Music:** Starts calm/neutral (S01), drops slightly grey (S02), lifts with warmth (S03), builds (S04–S05), peaks confidently (S06), resolves warmly (S07).
- **Colour palette:** Brand Sky `#4A90E2` · Brand Teal `#00B8A9` · Brand Amber `#FFD166` · Brand Rose `#FF8A65`
- **Child characters:** Three distinct children — teal (confident), amber (confused → engaged), rose (distracted → enthusiastic). Consistent across all scenes.
- **No real people, no stock footage.** 100% animated characters.
